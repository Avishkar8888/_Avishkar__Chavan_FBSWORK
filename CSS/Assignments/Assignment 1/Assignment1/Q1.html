Explore Kyoto, Japan

Kyoto is one of the most beautiful and historic cities in Japan. It is famous for its temples, gardens, and traditional culture.

Top Attractions

  • Fushimi Inari Shrine
  • Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion)
  • Arashiyama Bamboo Grove
  • Gion District

Best Time to Visit

The best times to visit Kyoto are during the cherry blossom season in spring and the colorful autumn leaves in fall.
