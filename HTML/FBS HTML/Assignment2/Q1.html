Cricket

Cricket is a bat-and-ball sport played between two teams of 11 players each. It's popular worldwide, especially in countries like India, England, Australia, Pakistan, South Africa, Sri Lanka, and the West Indies. Tennis

Tennis is a popular racket sport played between two players (singles) or two teams of two (doubles), where the objective is to hit the ball over the net into the opponent’s court. It is played on various surfaces like clay, grass, and hard courts, each affecting how the game is played. Players score points using a unique system: 15, 30, 40, and game, with six games needed to win a set. The sport features four major tournaments known as the Grand Slams—Australian Open, French Open, Wimbledon, and US Open. Legendary players include Roger Federer, Rafael Nadal, Novak Djokovic, Serena Williams, and Iga Świątek. Tennis requires agility, strength, strategy, and mental focus, making it both challenging and exciting to watch and play. Men's Legends: Roger Federer (Switzerland) – Graceful and dominant on all surfaces Rafael Nadal (Spain) – King of Clay Novak Djokovic (Serbia) – Most Grand Slam titles (as of 2025) Carlos Alcaraz (Spain) – Young rising star

Badminton

Badminton is a fast-paced racket sport played either one-on-one (singles) or two-on-two (doubles), where players hit a shuttlecock over a net using lightweight rackets. The aim is to land the shuttle in the opponent's court and prevent it from being returned. A match is played in the best of three games, with each game going up to 21 points. It is played on a rectangular indoor court, and players must be quick, agile, and strategic. Badminton is especially popular in countries like China, Indonesia, India, South Korea, and Denmark. Famous players include PV Sindhu, Lin Dan, Lee Chong Wei, and Carolina Marín. The sport is also a major event in the Olympics and various international tournaments.
